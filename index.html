Privacy Policy – Panino Factory

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Panino Factory is an idle game developed by Giuseppe Russo Coco. No account is required to play.

1. Game data on your device

Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. If you use Android's backup, the system may include this data in your own device backup under Google's policies; the developer has no access to it. You can delete all game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

2. Optional sponsor videos (advertising)

The game offers optional sponsor videos: you choose whether to watch one in exchange for an in-game bonus. Nothing plays unless you tap it. These videos are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd).

To show and measure ads, the AdMob service may collect and process data from your device, such as the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. This data is processed by Google under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form before any personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Options → "Ad privacy choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

3. What the developer collects

The developer does not collect, store or sell any personal data and does not run its own analytics.

4. Purchases

The app has no in-app purchases.

5. Children

Panino Factory is not directed at children under 13.

6. Changes

Any change to how the app handles data will be reflected in this policy, with a new date above.
